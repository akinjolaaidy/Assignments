Mini Biography: Senior Softare Engineer Aiden Benjamin

Aiden Benjamin was a Software Engineer, Teacher, author, and former President of Sierra leone .

Early Life

He was born in Freetown and grew up with curiosity, discipline, and love for learning.

Important Achievement

He played a major role in Sierra Leone space and missile programmes.

He is remembered as the People’s President because he inspired millions of students.

One value that made him special was lifelong learning.
